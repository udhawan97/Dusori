Dusori is a private research desk for certifications and general topics. It organizes the product around a question-to-brief loop:

1. **Ask** a question in ordinary language.
2. Let Dusori route the question locally, then allow relevant providers to **search and rank** a varied first shelf. Review and approve any additional results you want to keep.
3. **Read** available text locally, move through the source shelf, and carry exact passages into learner-owned notes.
4. Build a cited **brief** only from quotable passages.
5. Inspect the research trail in **Map**, as a linear **Outline** or a separated evidence atlas.

The four primary destinations stay fixed: **Research**, **Sources**, **Map**, and **Settings**. Research stays query-first, while roadmaps, objectives, reviews, and certification outline import remain optional under Settings.

## Trust contract

- Research saves its ranked first shelf automatically; further results require individual approval, and a URL reference still cannot support a claim until readable text exists.
- Removed sources stop contributing to counts, Map, quoted passages, and briefs until restored.
- Provider consent is separate by provider and names what leaves the device.
- A provider failure stays a failure in the durable trail; it is not rewritten as “nothing found.”
- Local search, graph construction, progress, and insights do not require AI or a hosted Dusori service.
- Nothing researches, installs, or restarts while Dusori is closed.

The hosted app cannot fetch arbitrary pages. The optional loopback companion can read an exact page when you choose **Read from _host_** and adds configured providers. A blocked response remains a browser-ready reference. The companion does not place its session credential in the browser URL, stdout, or logs. Optional AI is separately consented and model-labeled.
