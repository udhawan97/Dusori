Dusori is a private learning studio for certifications and general topics. It organizes the product around a daily loop:

1. **Continue** one evidence-backed next step.
2. **Read** a source you deliberately saved.
3. **Annotate** in ordinary Markdown.
4. Explicitly **Mark learned** or complete a review.
5. Inspect what changed in **Map**, as a galaxy or linear **Outline**.

The four primary destinations stay fixed: **Learn**, **Sources**, **Map**, and **Settings**. Research, roadmaps, evidence signals, and reviews are topic tools inside Learn—not separate places you have to memorize.

## Trust contract

- Previewing a result does not save it. **Save source** and **Save & read** do.
- Opening a topic, source, review, or map does not mark anything learned.
- Only your roadmap action changes objective progress. Only **Got it** or **Needs work** changes the review schedule.
- Provider consent is separate by provider and names what leaves the device.
- A provider failure stays a failure in the durable trail; it is not rewritten as “nothing found.”
- Local search, graph construction, progress, and insights do not require AI or a hosted Dusori service.
- Nothing researches, installs, restarts, or claims progress while Dusori is closed.

The hosted app cannot fetch arbitrary pages. The optional loopback companion can fetch an exact page only after confirmation and adds configured research providers. It does not place its session credential in the browser URL, stdout, or logs. Optional AI is separately consented, model-labeled, and always sits on top of a deterministic baseline.
