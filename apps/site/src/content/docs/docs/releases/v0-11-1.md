---
title: v0.11.1 release
description: Corrected consent wording, an honest network claim, and public surfaces that describe the research-first product.
---

A correctness release. No new capability ships here; what changed is that Dusori now tells you the truth about what it sends, and the public surfaces describe the product [v0.11.0](../v0-11-0/) actually made it.

## The AI permission now names everything it covers

Research has one AI permission. It has always granted three things together — ranked results, a research brief, and the synthesis overview — but its wording described only ranking, and said "nothing else from your workspace is sent".

That was written before the synthesis overview existed. Once it shipped, the overview could send up to sixty quoted passages under a permission that never mentioned passages at all. The dialog now names all three uses and what each one sends, so the grant matches the traffic.

Nothing about the traffic changed. The overview always sent only passages already quoted in that topic, and it still does.

## An honest claim under Understand this topic

**Understand this topic** said "Nothing here contacts the network." True for reading sources and for building the learning page, and true for the synthesis until you allow AI — at which point it was wrong.

The line is now conditional. With AI allowed it names the model the overview is sent to; without it, the original claim stands, because it is then correct.

The same correction was applied to the [Sources](../../sources/) page, which stated the blanket claim and then described the AI overview six lines later.

## The README's privacy list was wrong

It said optional AI receives "only the disclosed query plus candidate or accepted-source metadata". Three features send text from sources you already saved: the synthesis overview sends up to sixty quoted passages, a review session sends up to four 320-character excerpts, and tutor preferences send your own preference lines and the change you type. The list now says so, grouped by the three separate consents that govern them.

## Public surfaces that had not caught up

The landing page still opened "Learn deeply. Keep the files.", walked through the pre-v0.10.0 loop ending at spaced review, and listed capabilities under "Everything below is in the current build" without Synthesis or Learn. Its page title and social card carried the old positioning too. All of that now describes the research-first product, and both the README and the landing page lead with the research view, captured from the current build.

Also corrected:

- **Getting started** never mentioned **Read saved sources**, **Build synthesis**, or **Create learning page**. It now covers them, plus **Keep this topic fresh**, and ends with troubleshooting rather than a migration warning about v0.2.0.
- The [workspace file contract](../../workspaces/) omitted `Synthesis.md` and `Learning/learn.html`, and [conflict safety](../../conflict-safety/) omitted their two protection rules.
- [Browser support](../../browser-support/) said five keyless providers; seven ship, and Reddit and YouTube were missing from the companion list.
- The Stack Exchange provider is labeled **Stack Overflow** in the app, so the documentation now says Stack Overflow.
- The review-excerpt figure had been changed to eight; four is correct, matching the app's own consent dialog.
- The changelog had no comparison links for 0.10.0 or 0.11.0.

## Unchanged

Nothing runs while Dusori is closed. A topic you explicitly arm may re-scan itself when you open that topic after seven days, using only providers you already allowed. Reading arbitrary web pages still needs the local companion. AI remains optional, consent-gated, model-named, and always has a deterministic fallback.
