Released 2026-08-04.

v0.12.3 is the Research Desk release. It keeps the v0.12.2 desktop launch and icon repairs, then replaces the remaining maze of topic actions with five direct destinations: **Research**, **Sources**, **Learn**, **Map**, and **Settings**.

## Research in one place

- Ask a plain-language question; no roadmap or objective is required.
- Select providers from an initially unchecked consent sheet that says what may leave the device and what returned text may be saved.
- Dusori records `found`, `empty`, and `failed` per provider, ranks a varied shortlist, saves up to five results, reads available text, and builds a cited brief.
- The latest query and provider outcomes survive reload; a zero-result lookup does not reuse an older brief as its answer.
- Built-in and companion-backed providers now show **Ready**, **Requires the free local companion**, or a configuration reason before a run.

Provider-returned abstracts, extracts, and READMEs may be saved after consent. Dusori does not fetch arbitrary result pages automatically. URL-only results remain browser-ready references and cannot support a claim.

## Optional learning without the maze

The study path, official-outline import, roadmaps, review, objectives, and evidence insights have a first-class home under **Learn**. Certification intent survives relaunch, and AI-103, AI-901, and other Microsoft exam-code questions include Microsoft Learn among the suggested providers. None of these tools becomes a prerequisite for asking a question or building a research brief.

## Evidence integrity

- Sources show whether they are read evidence, readable evidence, references, or blocked pages.
- Provider origin, ranking reasons, and quoted-passage counts stay visible.
- A retry can add readable text to an existing URL reference without duplicating it.
- Any source or claim change marks the current synthesis stale.
- If a brief was edited, the edit stays active and the refresh becomes a proposal in **Needs attention**.
- Activity-log failures appear as warnings after a successful source commit.
- Removed sources disappear from Map, counts, health, and synthesis until restored.
- A blocked page keeps **Open original**; the desktop hands validated HTTP(S) links to the system browser.

## Desktop support

- macOS 12 or newer on Apple silicon only
- Windows x64
- Browser/PWA and Node.js 24 companion
- Signed GitHub updater artifacts with separate check, download, install, and relaunch actions

The Mac and Windows installers are open-source builds but are not Apple-notarized or Microsoft code-signed. Gatekeeper or SmartScreen may warn on first launch; compare the installer with `SHA256SUMS.txt` from the release.

:::caution[One-time repair for v0.12.0 and v0.12.1]
Those two desktop versions open to a launch-time 404, so Settings is inaccessible. Install v0.12.3 manually once. The workspace is stored separately and remains in place. Normal in-app updates work from v0.12.2 onward.
:::
